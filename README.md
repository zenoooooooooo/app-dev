## 📖 STORY OVERVIEW

*The Walking Dead* follows a world devastated by a zombie apocalypse.

The story begins with **Rick Grimes**, a sheriff's deputy who wakes up from a coma and discovers that civilization has collapsed.

He searches for his family and eventually joins other survivors struggling to stay alive.

However, the biggest danger isn't always the walkers. Sometimes, it's other human beings.

## ⭐ CHARACTER DATABASE

### 🧔 Rick Grimes
**Role:** Main protagonist

- Former sheriff's deputy.
- Natural-born leader.
- Will do anything to protect his family.
- Known for his determination and survival instincts.

### 🏹 Daryl Dixon
**Role:** Main character

- Expert hunter and tracker.
- Skilled with a crossbow.
- Loyal and protective.
- One of the franchise's most recognizable characters.

### ⚔️ Michonne
**Role:** Main character

- Expert swordswoman.
- Known for her katana.
- Strong, intelligent, and independent.

### 🩸 Negan
**Role:** Antagonist / Antihero

- Former leader of the Saviors.
- Known for his baseball bat, Lucille.
- Charismatic and intimidating.

### ❤️ Glenn Rhee
**Role:** Main character

- Resourceful survivor.
- Compassionate and courageous.
- One of the group's most beloved members.

### 🔥 Carol Peletier
**Role:** Main character

- Experienced survivor.
- Highly intelligent and strategic.
- Has one of the biggest character transformations in the series.
